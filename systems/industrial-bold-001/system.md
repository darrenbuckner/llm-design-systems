# Runway Industrial System

A high-impact marketing aesthetic that combines bold condensed typography with industrial photography. The system uses a striking neon yellow-green accent against dark photographic backgrounds, with soft lavender as a secondary accent. Designed for maximum visual impact and immediate attention capture.

## Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- Neon Accent: #D4FF00 — Electric yellow-green used for arrows, highlights, and directional elements
- Lavender Block: #E4B8FF — Soft purple used for geometric accent blocks and brand markers

**Neutral Colors:**
- Deep Black: #1A1A1A — Used for footer bars and solid backgrounds
- Pure White: #FFFFFF — Primary text color for maximum contrast on photos
- Photo Overlay: rgba(0,0,0,0.3) — Subtle darkening applied to imagery for text legibility

**Supporting Colors:**
- Warm Gray: #8B8680 — Concrete tones from photography, natural material reference
- Charcoal: #2D2D2D — Footer background, secondary dark surfaces

## Typography

**Font Families:**
- Display: Condensed sans-serif (similar to Impact, Anton, or Bebas Neue)
- Body: Clean sans-serif (similar to Inter, Helvetica Neue, or DM Sans)
- Logo: Custom geometric sans-serif, medium weight

**Type Scale:**
- Hero Display: 120-160px, 900 weight, 0.95 line-height, stacked layout
- Tagline: 18-20px, 400 weight, 1.4 line-height
- Logo Text: 24-28px, 700 weight, tracking 0.1em

**Hierarchy Rules:**
- Display text uses ALL CAPS with extreme weight for maximum impact
- Each word on its own line creates vertical rhythm and scan-ability
- Body text uses sentence case with lighter weight for contrast
- Text positioned to leave space for photographic content

**Text Treatment:**
- White text directly on photography
- No text shadows needed when photo has sufficient contrast
- Stacked word arrangement for vertical impact
- Left-aligned display type with generous leading

## Spacing System

**Base Unit:** 8px
**Scale:** 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 80, 120

**Common Patterns:**
- Display text line-height: ~0.95em (tightly stacked)
- Section padding: 48-64px
- Footer height: 80-100px
- Accent block size: 80x80px to 120x120px
- Arrow/graphic elements: 200-300px for major directional graphics

## Component Styles

### Accent Blocks
- Square or rectangular geometric shapes
- Solid fill with secondary color (lavender)
- Contains single character or icon
- Size: 80-120px square
- No border radius — sharp corners for industrial feel
- Used to anchor typography or serve as visual punctuation

### Directional Arrows
- Bold stroke weight: 12-16px
- Neon accent color (#D4FF00)
- Large scale: 200-400px height
- Sharp pointed ends
- Used as compositional elements, not just indicators

### Footer Bar
- Full-width horizontal band
- Dark background (#1A1A1A or #2D2D2D)
- Height: 80-100px
- Contains tagline on left, logo on right
- Vertical divider line between elements (1px, subtle gray)
- Text: light weight, 16-18px

### Text Overlays
- Direct placement on photography
- No background cards or containers
- Relies on photo composition for legibility zones
- White text, heavy weight provides own contrast

## Layout Principles

- **Full-bleed photography:** Images extend edge-to-edge
- **Grid structure:** Asymmetric, editorial layout
- **Text zones:** Positioned in darker/simpler areas of photo
- **Graphic overlays:** Bold elements placed in open spaces of composition
- **Vertical stacking:** Display text arranged one word per line
- **Footer anchor:** Consistent dark band at bottom grounds the composition

## Visual Effects

- **Border Radius:** 0px — All elements use sharp corners for industrial aesthetic
- **Shadows:** None — Relies on photographic depth and contrast
- **Borders:** Minimal — 1px dividers only in footer
- **Backgrounds:** Full-bleed photography with occasional solid color blocks
- **Transparency:** Subtle darkening overlays on photos when needed for text

## Graphic Elements

### Arrow Motif
- Oversized directional arrows as compositional elements
- Neon yellow-green (#D4FF00)
- Can extend beyond frame edges
- Stroke-based, not filled
- Points toward key content or creates movement

### Letter Blocks
- Single character inside colored square
- Lavender (#E4B8FF) background
- Dark text inside
- Functions as visual anchor and brand mark
- Sharp corners, geometric precision

## Photography Style

- Aerial/overhead perspective preferred
- Industrial or infrastructure subjects
- Neutral color palette (grays, tans, concrete tones)
- High detail and texture
- Compositional space left for text placement
- Natural lighting, documentary feel

## Design Tone

Bold, industrial, and confident. This system demands attention through scale and contrast rather than decoration. The combination of heavy condensed typography with neon accents against documentary photography creates a sense of innovation within established industries. The aesthetic feels both modern and grounded — forward-looking yet substantial.

## Usage Notes for AI Implementation

**When to use this system:**
- High-impact marketing landing pages
- Industrial or infrastructure brand communications
- Innovation announcements within traditional sectors
- Hero sections that need immediate attention capture

**Key implementation details:**
- Always use full-bleed photography as the foundation
- Position text in areas of the photo with sufficient contrast
- Stack display words vertically, one word per line
- Use neon accent sparingly — one major graphic element per composition
- Footer bar provides consistent anchoring across pages
- Maintain sharp corners throughout — no border radius

**Typography pairing:**
- Display: Use Anton, Bebas Neue, or similar condensed sans-serif
- Body: Use Inter, DM Sans, or system sans-serif
- Always ALL CAPS for display text

**Color application:**
- Neon yellow-green (#D4FF00) only for accent graphics (arrows, highlights)
- Lavender (#E4B8FF) for geometric blocks/brand elements
- Never use neon as text color — keep text white or black
- Photography provides the color depth and texture
