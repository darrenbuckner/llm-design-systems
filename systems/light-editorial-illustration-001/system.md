# Woodcut Editorial System

A stark, confident design system that pairs commanding serif typography with hand-drawn woodcut-style illustrations. The aesthetic evokes vintage editorial prints and philosophical manifestos—stripped of decoration, focused entirely on message and meaning.

## Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- Pure White: #FFFFFF — Primary background, creates maximum breathing room
- Pure Black: #000000 — Headlines, illustration lines, maximum impact

**Neutral Colors:**
- Surface/Background: #FFFFFF — Clean, uninterrupted canvas
- Text Primary: #000000 — Headlines and illustration
- Text Secondary: #4A4A4A — Body copy, softer reading experience
- Text Tertiary: #666666 — Attribution, metadata, subtle elements
- Borders: #E8E8E8 — Illustration containers, subtle separation

**Accent Colors:**
- This system intentionally avoids accent colors—the stark black/white contrast IS the accent
- Any color introduction would dilute the philosophical, timeless quality

## Typography

**Font Families:**
- Primary Headlines: Condensed Sans-Serif (similar to Impact, Bebas Neue, or Anton)
- Body Text: System serif or Georgia — warm, readable contrast to bold headlines
- Attribution: Clean sans-serif at small sizes

**Type Scale:**
- Display/Headline: 48-64px, Black (900) weight, tight tracking (-0.02em), title case
- Body Copy: 18-20px, Regular (400), generous line-height (1.6-1.7)
- Attribution/Meta: 14px, Medium (500), standard tracking

**Hierarchy Rules:**
- Headlines use ALL CAPS or Title Case with extreme weight contrast
- Body text is deliberately understated—regular weight, muted color
- Massive size differential between headline and body creates visual drama
- Single column layout centers attention on the message

## Spacing System

**Base Unit:** 8px
**Scale:** 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 80, 120

**Common Patterns:**
- Top padding to headline: 80-120px (generous vertical breathing room)
- Headline to body: 32-40px
- Body to illustration: 48-64px
- Illustration internal padding: 0 (edge-to-edge within frame)
- Bottom attribution spacing: 48-64px
- Container horizontal padding: 24-48px (responsive)

## Component Styles

### Headlines
- Weight: Black (900) or Extra Bold (800)
- Style: Condensed sans-serif
- Alignment: Centered
- Case: Title Case or ALL CAPS
- Color: Pure black #000000
- Line height: 1.0-1.1 (tight)
- Max width: 600-700px for readability

### Body Text Blocks
- Weight: Regular (400)
- Style: Serif for warmth and readability
- Alignment: Centered
- Color: Dark gray #4A4A4A (not pure black)
- Line height: 1.6-1.7
- Max width: 500-550px
- Sentence structure: Short, punchy, declarative

### Illustration Containers
- Border: 2-3px solid #000000
- Border radius: 8-12px (subtle softening)
- Background: White with stippled/halftone texture
- Aspect ratio: Roughly 4:5 or square
- Style: Woodcut/pen-and-ink aesthetic
- Content: Single figure, nature setting, contemplative mood

### Attribution Footer
- Layout: Two-column, left and right aligned
- Left: Author/source handle
- Right: Title/source in script or italic
- Color: #4A4A4A or #666666
- Size: 14px
- Separator: Subtle horizontal line or generous whitespace

## Layout Principles

- **Max content width:** 600-700px
- **Grid structure:** Single column, centered
- **Vertical rhythm:** Generous, almost meditative spacing
- **Alignment:** Strictly centered for headlines and body
- **Content density:** Extremely low—each element has room to breathe
- **Aspect ratio:** Vertical/portrait orientation (social media optimized)

## Visual Effects

- **Border Radius:** 8-12px on illustration containers only
- **Shadows:** None—completely flat design
- **Borders:** Thick (2-3px) black borders on illustrations only
- **Backgrounds:** Pure white, no gradients or patterns
- **Texture:** Within illustrations only (stippling, crosshatching, halftone dots)

## Illustration Style

- **Technique:** Pen-and-ink, woodcut aesthetic
- **Shading:** Stippling, crosshatching, parallel lines
- **Subject:** Single figure in nature, contemplative poses
- **Mood:** Peaceful, introspective, timeless
- **Color:** Black and white only, no grayscale fills
- **Detail level:** High detail in textures, simple in composition

## Design Tone

This system projects confidence, intentionality, and timelessness. The extreme minimalism and high contrast communicate that the message needs no embellishment—it stands on its own merit. The hand-drawn illustration style adds warmth and humanity, preventing the stark typography from feeling cold. Overall aesthetic: philosophical manifesto meets vintage editorial print.

## Usage Notes for AI Implementation

**When to use this system:**
- Inspirational or motivational content
- Philosophy, wisdom, life principles
- Personal development messaging
- Editorial quotes and manifestos
- Content meant to feel timeless and authoritative

**Key implementation rules:**
1. Resist the urge to add color—the power is in the restraint
2. Headlines must be genuinely bold (900 weight minimum)
3. Body copy should use short, declarative sentences
4. Always include generous vertical whitespace
5. Illustrations should feel hand-crafted, not digitally generated
6. Center everything—this is not a layout for asymmetry
7. Keep content density extremely low
8. Portrait orientation works best for social sharing

**Typography pairing:**
- If using Google Fonts: Anton or Bebas Neue for headlines, Lora or Merriweather for body
- The contrast between geometric sans and organic serif is essential

**Common mistakes to avoid:**
- Adding accent colors (breaks the philosophical gravitas)
- Using too many text elements (dilutes impact)
- Centering very long paragraphs (keep body text brief)
- Using thin or light weight headlines (loses commanding presence)
- Adding decorative elements beyond the illustration
