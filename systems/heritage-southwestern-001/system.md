# Trailhead Heritage System

A design system rooted in southwestern heritage patterns, combining deep teal-slate backgrounds with warm terracotta accents and cream surfaces. The aesthetic evokes vintage field guides, woven textiles, and outdoor adventure with a distinctly American craft tradition. Features bold geometric patterns, textured surfaces, and a strong sense of handmade authenticity.

## Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- Slate Teal: #3A5159 — Primary background, creates depth and sophistication
- Terracotta: #C4673A — Primary accent, warm and earthy, used for decorative elements
- Cream: #E8DCC4 — Primary surface color, warm white for content areas

**Neutral Colors:**
- Deep Slate: #2C3E42 — Darker variant for text and emphasis
- Warm Black: #2A3438 — Primary text on light backgrounds
- Muted Teal: #4A6169 — Secondary background, lighter variant

**Accent Colors:**
- Burnt Sienna: #A85532 — Darker terracotta variant for depth
- Sand: #D4C9B5 — Muted cream for subtle backgrounds
- Forest: #344A50 — Deep accent for borders and lines

**State Colors:**
- Success: #5C7A5E — Muted sage green
- Warning: #C4673A — Uses primary terracotta
- Error: #8B3D2A — Deep rust red

## Typography

**Font Families:**
- Display: Condensed serif with strong vertical stress (Oswald, Bebas Neue, or similar condensed sans)
- Primary: Classic serif with moderate contrast (Playfair Display, Libre Baskerville)
- Body: Clean serif or humanist sans for readability

**Type Scale:**
- Display: 72px, 700 weight, 0.9 line-height, uppercase, tight letter-spacing (-0.02em)
- Heading 1: 48px, 700 weight, 1.1 line-height, uppercase
- Heading 2: 32px, 600 weight, 1.2 line-height, title case
- Heading 3: 24px, 600 weight, 1.3 line-height
- Body: 16px, 400 weight, 1.6 line-height
- Caption: 14px, 400 weight, 1.5 line-height
- Small: 12px, 500 weight, 1.4 line-height, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.1em

**Hierarchy Rules:**
- Display text uses condensed uppercase for maximum impact
- Mixed case hierarchy: uppercase for display/labels, title case for headings, sentence case for body
- Strong weight contrast between headings (700) and body (400)
- Generous letter-spacing on small uppercase text

## Visual Effects

**Border Radius:**
- All elements: 0 (sharp corners throughout)
- Exceptions: None — geometric precision is core to aesthetic

**Shadows:**
- Minimal use — flat design predominates
- When used: Hard-edge geometric shadows (8px offset, no blur)
- Shadow color: Terracotta at 30% opacity or slate at 20%

**Borders:**
- Thickness: 2px standard, 3-4px emphasis
- Style: Solid only
- Decorative: Geometric step patterns, zigzag borders

**Backgrounds:**
- Textured: Canvas/linen texture overlay at 5-10% opacity
- Solid: Deep slate-teal as primary
- Patterns: Southwestern geometric in terracotta and cream
- Gradients: Not typically used

**Decorative Elements:**
- Triangle/chevron patterns
- Diamond shapes
- Step/stair patterns
- Symmetrical geometric compositions
- Mountain/peak silhouettes

## Design Tone

This system embodies rugged authenticity and heritage craftsmanship. It feels handmade yet precise, warm yet sophisticated. The combination of deep slate-teal with terracotta creates a palette that feels both outdoorsy and refined — like a well-worn field guide or a quality wool blanket. The geometric patterns reference southwestern textile traditions while maintaining modern usability.

The overall impression is trustworthy, timeless, and distinctly American in its outdoor heritage aesthetic. It works well for brands focused on outdoor adventure, artisanal goods, craft traditions, or heritage storytelling.

## Usage Notes for AI Implementation

When implementing this design system:

1. **Embrace sharp corners:** Avoid border-radius entirely — the geometric precision is essential to the aesthetic
2. **Use texture sparingly:** A subtle canvas texture adds authenticity without overwhelming
3. **Geometric accents:** Add triangular or diamond decorative elements to headers and section breaks
4. **Color balance:** Use slate-teal for 60% of the design, cream for 30%, terracotta for 10%
5. **Typography hierarchy:** Use condensed uppercase for display, classic serif for body
6. **Pattern integration:** Geometric patterns should frame content, not compete with it
7. **Maintain warmth:** Despite the structured geometry, the palette keeps everything feeling inviting
8. **Whitespace:** Generous padding within cream content areas creates breathing room
