# Tidal Wellness System

A serene, nature-inspired design system that combines watercolor aesthetics with warm earth tones. This system evokes feelings of calm, balance, and organic growth—perfect for wellness, coaching, and personal development platforms. The design balances professional credibility with approachable warmth through its use of teal accents, coral highlights, and soft cream backgrounds.

## Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- Primary Teal: #1A5F5A — Main brand color, used for headers, navigation, and key UI elements
- Coral Accent: #E86B4A — Call-to-action buttons, highlights, and emphasis points
- Warm Orange: #F7A659 — Secondary accent, used in illustrations and decorative elements

**Neutral Colors:**
- Cream Background: #F5F0E8 — Primary page background, creates warmth
- Pure White: #FFFFFF — Card backgrounds, content areas
- Soft Gray: #F8F6F2 — Subtle section differentiation

**Text Colors:**
- Dark Slate: #2C3E50 — Primary text, headings
- Medium Gray: #4A5568 — Body text
- Muted Gray: #6B7B8A — Secondary text, captions, metadata

**State Colors:**
- Success: #38A169 — Confirmation, positive feedback
- Warning: #D69E2E — Caution states
- Error: #E53E3E — Error messages, validation

**Decorative Colors (from illustrations):**
- Ocean Blue: #4A90A4 — Watercolor accents
- Soft Teal: #5BA3A0 — Gradient elements
- Peach: #F5B89A — Warm illustration tones
- Lavender: #9B8EC4 — Accent in artwork

## Typography

**Font Families:**
- Primary Headings: Playfair Display (serif) — Elegant, editorial feel for main headlines
- Secondary Headings: System serif or Georgia — Fallback for subheadings
- Body Text: Inter or system sans-serif — Clean, highly readable for body content
- Accent Text: Handwritten style for special callouts (optional decorative use)

**Type Scale:**
- Display/Hero: 42px, 700 weight, 1.2 line-height — Main page headlines
- Heading 1: 32px, 700 weight, 1.3 line-height — Section titles
- Heading 2: 24px, 600 weight, 1.4 line-height — Subsection headers
- Heading 3: 18px, 600 weight, 1.4 line-height — Card titles, feature names
- Body Large: 18px, 400 weight, 1.7 line-height — Lead paragraphs
- Body: 16px, 400 weight, 1.7 line-height — Standard body text
- Small/Caption: 14px, 400 weight, 1.5 line-height — Metadata, footer text
- Micro: 12px, 500 weight, 1.4 line-height — Labels, tags

**Hierarchy Rules:**
- Headlines use serif fonts for warmth and sophistication
- Body text uses sans-serif for readability
- Italics used for emphasis and quotes in an editorial style
- Headlines in dark slate (#2C3E50), body in medium gray (#4A5568)

## Design Tone

This system embodies warmth, authenticity, and professional calm. It feels like a conversation with a trusted advisor—approachable yet credible. The watercolor illustrations add an artistic, human touch that contrasts beautifully with the clean typography. The overall aesthetic suggests personal growth, balance, and mindful progress without feeling clinical or corporate.

## Usage Notes for AI Implementation

1. **Hero sections** should feature large watercolor-style illustrations or nature photography with overlaid text. Keep text brief and impactful.

2. **Service offerings** work best as cards with icons or small illustrations, short headlines, and 2-3 lines of description.

3. **Typography pairing** is crucial: Use Playfair Display (or similar serif) for headlines to create warmth, and Inter (or similar sans-serif) for body text for readability.

4. **Color usage**: Teal (#1A5F5A) for trust and professionalism, coral (#E86B4A) sparingly for CTAs and emphasis. Cream backgrounds create the signature warmth.

5. **Whitespace** is generous—let content breathe. Avoid cramped layouts.

6. **Images** should feel authentic and warm: real people, nature scenes, candid moments rather than staged stock photography.

7. **Mobile experience** prioritizes readability and easy navigation. Stack elements vertically, increase touch targets, maintain generous padding.

8. **Form elements** should feel inviting, not clinical. Rounded corners, subtle focus states, and encouraging microcopy.

9. **Avoid** harsh contrasts, corporate blues, or overly geometric patterns that would conflict with the organic aesthetic.

10. **Newsletter sections** work well as a simple row: brief headline, email input, and coral subscribe button.
