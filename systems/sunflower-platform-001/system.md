# Sunflower Platform System

A bold, energetic design system built around vibrant yellow backgrounds with high-contrast black typography and playful interactive elements.

## Color Palette

Primary Colors:
- Primary Yellow: #FFD700 — Main background color, primary brand expression
- Black: #000000 — Primary text, icons, and high-contrast elements

Neutral Colors:
- Surface/Background: #FFD700 (primary) / #FFFFFF (secondary)
- Text Primary: #000000
- Text Secondary: #2C2C2C
- Borders: #000000

Accent/State Colors:
- Active State: #000000 on #FFD700
- Hover State: Slightly darker yellow (#F7CA00)
- Contrast Element: #000000 with fold/shadow effects

## Typography

Font Families:
- Primary: Serif (likely Times or similar classical serif)
- Secondary: Sans-serif for UI elements

Type Scale:
- Large Display: 120px+, Bold, 1.0 line-height
- Heading 1: 48px, Regular, 1.2 line-height
- Body/UI: 16px, Regular, 1.4 line-height
- Small/Caption: 14px, Regular, 1.3 line-height

Hierarchy Rules:
- Numbers and key elements use oversized serif typography
- Body text maintains classical serif styling
- UI elements like buttons use clean sans-serif
- High contrast between text and background ensures readability

## Spacing System

Base Unit: 8px
Scale: 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128

Common Patterns:
- Component padding: 16-24px
- Large element spacing: 48-64px
- Container margins: 32px
- Button padding: 12px 24px

## Component Styles

### Buttons
- Style: Pill-shaped with rounded corners
- Border: 2px solid black
- Background: Transparent on yellow
- Text: Black, sans-serif, medium weight
- Padding: 12px 32px
- Border radius: 24px (full pill shape)
- Hover: Slight shadow or background tint

### Cards/Containers
- Background: Solid yellow (#FFD700)
- Border: 2-3px solid black
- Border radius: 8px
- Shadow: Black folded corner effect (bottom-right)
- Padding: 48px internally

### Interactive Elements
- Cursor: Custom pointer with white fill and black outline
- Hover states: Subtle shadow or slight color shift
- Focus: Black border emphasis

## Layout Principles

- Centered content alignment
- Generous white space around elements
- Square/rectangular proportions with slight rounding
- Content hierarchy through size rather than color
- Playful paper-like fold effects for depth

## Visual Effects

- Border Radius: 8px for containers, 24px for pills/buttons
- Shadows: Paper fold effect (triangular black corner)
- Borders: 2-3px solid black throughout
- Backgrounds: Solid colors, no gradients
- Effects: Paper-like folded corners, high contrast outlines

## Design Tone

Bold, playful, and confident with a retro-modern aesthetic. The design embraces high contrast and vibrant colors while maintaining excellent readability. The paper fold effects and classical typography give it a tactile, almost analog feeling despite being digital.

## Usage Notes for AI Implementation

Use this system for applications requiring high energy and visibility. Perfect for creative tools, educational platforms, or consumer-facing products. Maintain the high contrast ratios for accessibility. The yellow background should dominate with black providing all contrast and definition. Keep interactions simple and direct with clear visual feedback.
