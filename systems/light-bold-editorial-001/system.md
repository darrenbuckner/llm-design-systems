# Bold Editorial Voice

Bold, confident, sophisticated design system with earthy tones and strong typography. Creating massive visual impact through high-contrast text, striking imagery, and memorable compositions that feel human, relatable, and decidedly not corporate.

## Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- Olive/Sage Green: #7C7C68 — Muted olive for backgrounds, hero sections, brand moments representing groundedness, natural sophistication, calm confidence
- Crisp White: #FFFFFF — Typography (especially large headlines), contrast elements, clean sections representing clarity, boldness, modern simplicity
- Deep Charcoal: #2A2A2A — Body text, secondary backgrounds, depth representing sophistication, seriousness, grounding

**Secondary/Accent Colors:**
- Warm Coral/Terracotta: #E17B5F — CTAs, highlights, energetic moments representing energy, warmth, creativity
- Sunset Orange: #F39C63 — Secondary accents, illustrations, dynamic elements
- Teal/Turquoise: #5BA8A0 — Links, interactive elements, supporting graphics representing science, knowledge, clarity
- Magenta/Pink: #D75C7C — Creative accents, illustrations, playful moments

**Neutral Palette:**
- Off-White: #F8F8F5 — Subtle backgrounds
- Light Gray: #E5E5E0 — Dividers, subtle sections
- Mid Gray: #8C8C7A — Secondary text, muted elements
- Warm Beige: #D4CFC0 — Alternative backgrounds
- Black: #000000 — High contrast moments, bold statements

**Usage Guidelines:**
- Backgrounds: Primarily olive/sage or off-white for warmth
- Typography: White for heroes on dark backgrounds, charcoal for body text
- Accents: Use coral/terracotta sparingly for high-impact moments
- Contrast: Embrace bold contrast - large white text on olive is signature
- Balance: 70% neutrals, 20% primary (olive), 10% accents

## Typography

**Font Families:**
- Primary Display Font: Ultra-Bold Sans-Serif (Impact, Bebas Neue, Druk, Anton, Archivo Black) — Heavy weight, condensed or regular width, commanding presence for headlines, hero text, brand statements
- Secondary Headline Font: Bold Sans-Serif (Helvetica Bold, Inter Bold, DM Sans Bold) — Clean, strong, highly readable for section headers, subheadings, navigation
- Body Font: Clean Sans-Serif (Inter, Helvetica, SF Pro, Open Sans) — Excellent readability, professional, modern for body text, descriptions, UI elements

**Type Scale & Hierarchy:**
- H1 (Hero Display): 72-120px, Black/Heavy (900), 0.9-1.0 line-height, -0.02em letter-spacing, UPPERCASE — Main hero headlines, brand statements
- H2 (Major Header): 48-64px, Extra Bold (800-900), 1.0-1.1 line-height, -0.01em letter-spacing, UPPERCASE or Title Case — Section titles, major statements
- H3 (Section Header): 32-40px, Bold (700), 1.1-1.2 line-height — Subsections, feature titles
- H4 (Component Header): 24-28px, Bold (700), 1.3 line-height — Card headers, list titles
- Body Large: 20-22px, Regular-Medium (400-500), 1.5 line-height — Lead paragraphs, important descriptions
- Body: 16-18px, Regular (400), 1.6 line-height — Standard text, descriptions
- Body Small: 14px, Regular (400), 1.5 line-height — Captions, metadata, fine print
- Subtitle/Descriptor: 14-16px, Medium-Semibold (500-600), 1.4 line-height, 0.1em letter-spacing, UPPERCASE — Eyebrow text, category labels, subtitles

**Typography Guidelines:**
- Display Text: Always bold, often uppercase, create massive visual impact
- Hierarchy: Extreme size contrast between display and body text
- Spacing: Tight leading on headlines, generous on body text
- Alignment: Left-aligned body text, centered or left for display based on context
- Contrast: Use white text on dark backgrounds for maximum impact

## Spacing System

**Base Unit:** 8px
**Scale:** 2, 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128, 160

**Spacing Applications:**
- Hero Sections: Generous 96-160px vertical padding
- Section Spacing: 64-96px between major sections
- Component Padding: 24-48px internal padding
- Element Margins: 16-32px between related elements
- Tight Spacing: 8-16px for closely related items

## Component Styles

### Buttons

**Primary Button:**
- Background: Coral/terracotta (#E17B5F)
- Text: White
- Border Radius: 0px (sharp corners) OR 4px (subtle)
- Padding: 16px 40px
- Font Weight: 700
- Font Size: 16-18px
- Letter Spacing: 0.05em
- Transform: Uppercase
- Hover: Darken 15%, subtle lift
- Style: Bold and decisive, not playful

**Secondary Button:**
- Background: Transparent
- Border: 2px solid Charcoal or White (context-dependent)
- Text: Charcoal or White
- Border Radius: 0px or 4px
- Padding: 14px 38px
- Font Weight: 700
- Hover: Fill with border color, invert text

**Tertiary/Text Button:**
- Background: None
- Text: Coral or Teal with underline
- Font Weight: 600
- Hover: Opacity 0.7 or slight color shift

### Cards

**Standard Card:**
- Background: White or Off-white
- Border: 1px solid light gray OR no border with shadow
- Border Radius: 0px (sharp) or 8px (subtle)
- Padding: 32-48px
- Shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.1) or none for flat design
- Style: Clean and architectural

**Feature Card:**
- Larger scale (400px+ width)
- Bold header text (24-32px)
- Image or icon at top (coral/teal accent)
- Description (16px)
- Optional CTA link

### Navigation
- Clean, minimal navigation bar
- Bold font weight (700)
- Text links or pill-style active states
- White or off-white background
- Sticky/fixed positioning optional

### Hero Section
- Full-width colored backgrounds (olive/sage)
- Asymmetric layouts with subject on one side
- Large, bold typography on opposite side
- Minimal additional elements for maximum impact
- Subject can bleed to edge or extend beyond content area

## Layout Principles

**Grid System:**
- Columns: 12-column responsive grid
- Max Width: 1440px for full layouts, 1200px for text content
- Breakpoints: Mobile (320-767px), Tablet (768-1023px), Desktop (1024-1439px), Wide (1440px+)

**Layout Patterns:**

**Hero/Feature Sections:**
- Full-width colored backgrounds (olive/sage)
- Asymmetric layouts with subject on one side
- Large, bold typography on opposite side
- Minimal additional elements for maximum impact

**Content Sections:**
- Clean, spacious layouts
- Generous white space between sections
- Off-white or white backgrounds for readability
- Clear visual separation via background color shifts

**Split Layouts:**
- 50/50 or 60/40 splits common
- Image on one side, content on other
- Can reverse on alternating sections
- Maintains breathing room around both elements

## Visual Effects

**Border Radius:**
- Option A (Sharp & Modern): 0px border radius, completely sharp corners
- Option B (Sophisticated & Subtle): 4-8px border radius, subtle softening
- Recommendation: Start with Option A for maximum brand impact

**Shadows:**
- Level 1 (Subtle): 0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)
- Level 2 (Medium): 0 8px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.15)
- Use sparingly or not at all for flat design

**Borders:**
- 1-2px solid for buttons and cards when used
- Strategic use for definition without clutter

**Backgrounds:**
- Solid color blocks (olive, white, off-white)
- High-quality professional photography
- No patterns or textures typically
- Clean backgrounds for portraits

**Photography:**
- High-quality portraits with clean backgrounds
- Professional, well-lit compositions
- Subjects can extend beyond content containers
- Confident, authentic poses

## Design Tone

Bold, confident, and sophisticated with earthy, grounded aesthetics. This system bridges intellectual credibility with contemporary style through commanding typography, generous whitespace, and authentic personality. The design feels modern and professional while maintaining approachability. More bold and confident than typical professional sites, more sophisticated than typical entertainment sites.

## Usage Notes for AI Implementation

**Core Principles:**
- Lead with Bold Typography: Start designs with impactful headlines
- Embrace Asymmetry: Don't center everything - create dynamic layouts
- Use Color Strategically: Olive backgrounds for heroes, white/off-white for content
- Maximize Contrast: White text on dark = signature look
- Keep It Clean: Resist urge to fill space - let designs breathe
- Sharp or Subtle: Choose between sharp corners (modern) or minimal rounding (4-8px)
- Photography Matters: High-quality portraits with clean backgrounds essential

**Quick Reference:**
- Hero: Olive background (#7C7C68), huge white uppercase headline (96px+), asymmetric layout
- Content: Off-white background (#F8F8F5), charcoal text, generous spacing (64px+)
- Cards: White background, sharp or 8px radius, 40px padding, coral accent CTAs
- Buttons: Coral (#E17B5F) primary, 16px padding vertical, uppercase, 700 weight

**Avoid:**
- Playful or overly decorative fonts
- Overcrowding layouts with too many elements
- Bright, saturated colors as primary palette
- Small, timid typography
- Low-quality or casual photography
- Losing the sophisticated, grounded aesthetic
