# Opportunity Blue System

A confident, campaign-driven design system built around a bold royal blue signature color. Features high-impact condensed typography, strong horizontal bands, and an editorial aesthetic that pairs vibrant lifestyle photography with declarative messaging. The system communicates ambition, empowerment, and forward momentum.

## Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- Primary Blue: #0072CE — Signature brand blue, used for hero banners and primary CTAs
- Pure White: #FFFFFF — Primary text on blue, clean backgrounds

**Neutral Colors:**
- Rich Black: #1A1A1A — Headlines on photography, high-contrast text
- Charcoal: #2D2D2D — Body text, secondary information
- Light Gray: #F5F5F5 — Background surfaces, card backgrounds
- Medium Gray: #6B6B6B — Captions, metadata

**Accent Colors:**
- Vibrant Magenta: #8B2D8B — Accent photography element, creative highlights
- Heritage Red: #C41E3A — Secondary accent, emphasis moments
- Kelly Green: #2E8B57 — Tertiary accent, success states
- Warm Gold: #D4AF37 — Premium highlights, achievement indicators

**State Colors:**
- Success: #2E8B57
- Warning: #E5A000
- Error: #C41E3A
- Info: #0072CE

## Typography

**Font Families:**
- Primary Headings: Condensed sans-serif (similar to Oswald, Barlow Condensed, or Anton)
- Secondary/Body: Clean geometric sans-serif (similar to Montserrat or Gotham)
- Accent: Medium-weight tracking-wide sans for taglines

**Type Scale:**
- Display/Hero: 72-96px, 800 weight, condensed, uppercase, tight tracking (-0.02em), line-height 0.95
- Heading 1: 48-64px, 700 weight, condensed, uppercase, line-height 1.0
- Heading 2: 32-40px, 600 weight, condensed, uppercase, line-height 1.1
- Heading 3: 24-28px, 600 weight, mixed case, line-height 1.2
- Body Large: 18px, 400 weight, line-height 1.6
- Body: 16px, 400 weight, line-height 1.5
- Caption/Tagline: 14px, 500 weight, uppercase, wide tracking (0.15em), line-height 1.4

**Hierarchy Rules:**
- Headlines use condensed uppercase for maximum impact and minimal space
- Generous letter-spacing on taglines creates premium feel
- High contrast between headline weight and body text
- Period punctuation on headlines adds declarative tone

## Spacing System

**Base Unit:** 8px
**Scale:** 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128

**Common Patterns:**
- Hero section padding: 64-96px vertical
- Section spacing: 48-64px between major sections
- Component internal padding: 16-24px
- Text block spacing: 24px between headline and tagline
- Banner height: Fixed proportional bands (approximately 15-20% of viewport for footer banners)

## Component Styles

### Buttons

**Primary Button:**
- Background: #0072CE
- Text: #FFFFFF
- Font: 14-16px, 600 weight, uppercase, tracking 0.1em
- Padding: 16px 32px
- Border-radius: 0px (sharp corners for bold aesthetic) or 4px maximum
- Hover: Background darkens to #005BA1
- Active: Background #004C8C

**Secondary Button:**
- Background: transparent
- Border: 2px solid #0072CE
- Text: #0072CE
- Hover: Background #0072CE, text #FFFFFF

**Ghost Button (on dark/image backgrounds):**
- Background: transparent
- Border: 2px solid #FFFFFF
- Text: #FFFFFF
- Hover: Background rgba(255,255,255,0.15)

### Cards

**Standard Card:**
- Background: #FFFFFF
- Border: none or 1px solid #E5E5E5
- Border-radius: 0px-4px
- Shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)
- Padding: 24-32px
- Image aspect: 16:9 or 4:3

**Feature Card:**
- Full-bleed photography
- Text overlay with gradient scrim
- Sharp corners
- High contrast text

### Hero Sections

**Photo Hero:**
- Full-bleed lifestyle photography
- Overlay text in white or black depending on image
- Text positioned with generous padding
- Optional gradient scrim: linear-gradient(to bottom, rgba(0,0,0,0.3), transparent)

**Banner Strip:**
- Solid #0072CE background
- White uppercase text, centered
- Fixed height (80-120px)
- Acts as footer or section divider

### Navigation

- Clean horizontal layout
- Uppercase labels, wide tracking
- Minimal styling, relies on typography
- Active state: underline or color change

## Layout Principles

- **Max Content Width:** 1200-1400px
- **Grid Structure:** 12-column grid, 24px gutters
- **Full-bleed Sections:** Photography and color bands extend edge-to-edge
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Strong horizontal bands create structure
- **Alignment:** Center-aligned headlines, left-aligned body content
- **Responsive:** Stack to single column on mobile, maintain bold typography scale

## Visual Effects

- **Border Radius:** 0px (sharp) to 4px maximum — bold, confident aesthetic
- **Shadows:** Subtle when used — 0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)
- **Borders:** Rarely used; clean edges preferred
- **Photography Style:** Vibrant, high-saturation lifestyle imagery with confident subjects
- **Color Blocks:** Solid fills, no gradients on brand elements
- **Texture:** Comes from photography rather than UI patterns

## Design Tone

Bold, declarative, and empowering. This system speaks with confidence through condensed uppercase headlines that make strong statements. The royal blue signature color conveys trust, opportunity, and professionalism while vibrant accent colors (magenta, red, green) add creative energy. The aesthetic bridges corporate credibility with creative ambition — appropriate for economic development, entrepreneurship programs, or opportunity-focused campaigns.

## Usage Notes for AI Implementation

1. **Typography is the hero:** Let condensed uppercase headlines dominate. Keep them short and punchy (2-4 words per line maximum).

2. **Blue is sacred:** Use #0072CE consistently as the primary brand element. Don't dilute with too many competing colors.

3. **Sharp corners signal confidence:** Avoid rounded corners above 4px. This system is assertive, not soft.

4. **Horizontal bands create structure:** Use solid color strips to divide content and anchor layouts.

5. **Photography does heavy lifting:** Lifestyle imagery should be vibrant, diverse, and aspirational. Subjects should appear confident and successful.

6. **Wide tracking on taglines:** Uppercase taglines need 0.1-0.15em letter-spacing to breathe.

7. **Declarative punctuation:** Headlines can end with periods for added emphasis ("INSPIRING INNOVATION.")

8. **Minimize UI chrome:** Let content speak. Reduce borders, shadows, and decorative elements.
